### Summary

The U4 Catalogue functional design is architecturally sound: all eight FR3.1–FR3.8 requirements map to explicit BR3.x rules; WF1–WF6 align cleanly with Bolt sequence C1–C4 and IPC channels C-01–C-03; seller cost masking (BR3.17) is enforced at three distinct layers (IPC projection, SensitiveDataGuard, DOM non-mounting); CR-02/BR3.8 constraints are explicitly propagated through the OCR adapter contract (C-03/C-04); the append-only, no-float, outbox-in-same-transaction invariants are all respected. The one Major finding (unresolved category management decision that was flagged as blocking in the inception contract) prevents a developer from implementing the category picker without an assumption; it does not block the four-field minimum creation path and can be resolved with a single documented decision. Four Minor findings address definition gaps (SellingUnit ER completeness, series-mode activation, PreviewRow type, duplicate IPC mode ambiguity) that a developer would have to resolve by guessing today.
